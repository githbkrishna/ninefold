Questions

The things people ask before signing.

Still curious? Send a note — we answer within a working day.

What does a project usually cost? +

Retainers start around ₹1.8L / $2,500 a month for a single channel and scale with scope. Project work — a store build or a technical SEO overhaul — is quoted as a fixed fee after the audit.

How quickly can we start? +

Typically within two weeks. We keep one slot open each quarter so onboarding never means waiting on a queue.

Do you work with in-house teams? +

Often. We can run a channel end-to-end, or plug into your marketing lead as the specialist arm and hand over playbooks as we go.

Who actually does the work? +

The two people you meet in the first call — plus a designer and an analyst. No account manager relay race, no juniors on your budget.

What if it isn't working? +

After the first 90 days it's month-to-month. If the numbers aren't there, you leave with everything we built — accounts, docs, creative files.
